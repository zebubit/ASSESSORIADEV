1Connect your account with Facebook Login

The business owner signs in with Facebook and grants this app access to the Facebook Page and the Instagram professional account linked to it. Requested permissions: pages_show_list, pages_manage_metadata, pages_read_engagement, instagram_basic, instagram_manage_messages, instagram_manage_comments.

2Select the Instagram account to manage

These are the Pages you granted access to, with the Instagram professional account connected to each one. Choose the account whose messages and comments you want to answer.

3Answer your customers

"Direct messages" lists the Instagram Direct conversations of the selected account (instagram_manage_messages). Pick a conversation, type a reply and press Send: the message is delivered in the customer's Instagram app. "Comments" lists recent posts and lets you reply to each comment publicly (instagram_manage_comments).

Select a conversation on the left.
